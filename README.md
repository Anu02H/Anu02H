## 🧭 About Me

I am a **PhD Scholar in Ancient History at the University of Calcutta**, working at the intersection of history, archaeology, art history, epigraphy, iconography, and musicology.

My research focuses on the **cultural history of Odisha**, exploring how textual, visual, architectural, and archaeological evidence can be brought together to reconstruct the past.

**Research interests:**
- 📜 Epigraphy and historical sources
- 🛕 Temple architecture and sculpture
- 🎨 Art and iconography
- 🎵 History of music and musical instruments
- 🏺 Archaeology and material culture
- 🪙 Numismatics
- 🌊 Underwater archaeology

## 🎵 Current Research

### From Chant to Orchestra
**Epigraphic Evidence of Music and Musical Instruments in Odisha (c. 300–1190 CE)**

My doctoral research examines the epigraphic evidence of music and musical instruments in Odisha between approximately 300 and 1190 CE, focusing on:

- 🎵 Musical instruments
- 👤 Musicians and performers
- 🎼 Musical ensembles
- 🛕 Temple performances
- 🏛️ Religious institutions
- 👑 Patronage
- 📜 Epigraphic records
- 🎭 Performance traditions
